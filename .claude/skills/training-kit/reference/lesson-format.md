# Lesson format — the shape of Matthew's own trainings

THIS REPO IS PUBLIC. This file holds structure only. The evidence behind it — what he wrote, what he
changed, his words on quality — lives outside git in `~/.icmb-training/lesson-format.local.md`; read that
too when it exists. Never commit lesson text, his stories, time entries or prices.

Source: the live Classroom, read from his logged-in tab on 2026-10-04, compared with the agent-written
packs of 2026-10-02. He rewrote almost every lesson in Skool the day after the agents pasted them.
**His live Classroom text is newer than any pack or Doc: never overwrite it.** Whether the Classroom is
formally the source of record is his decision (see Open).

## The shape he keeps
1. **Title** — a plain concept or process name, no number. He retitles agent drafts; never key on a title.
2. **Expected Lesson Outcome:** one sentence: what the member has at the end. (His label, not "What you
   will finish".)
3. **His concept section** — replaces a generic "What this is". Named for the idea (a "… Methodology",
   "Background", "My Rules"). Short lines, often nested lists. The lesson's value is here, and it is his.
4. **Pro Tip:** one block, set off with those words, usually built on something he said.
5. **Do this** — numbered steps the member does now, with blanks to fill. 4–6 steps.
6. **You are done when** — two or three checks the member can verify alone.
7. **Post your check-in (in the community)** — two or three fill-in lines.
8. **Resources** — one "Worksheet (make a copy)" link ending in `/copy`, plus any book or video he names.

Typical length 230–500 words; his long ones run 730–980.

## What he changes when he rewrites an agent draft
- Retitles to the concept.
- Adds first-person story, rules and opinions. Only he can write these.
- Switches from third-person narration about him to "I" / "we", and talks to the member directly. The
  agent lines that survive are direct quotes and the Do-this steps.
- Adds a Methodology section and a Pro Tip where the draft only described.
- Links lessons to the other modules.
- Adds candid asides about what he has not tried yet.
- Writes whole lessons himself, with no skeleton, when the subject is his method end to end.
- Removes lessons he does not want, and adds video to some.

## What a draft must therefore do
- Draft sections 2, 5, 6, 7 and 8 fully — these survive his edit mostly intact.
- Draft section 3 only from his recorded words (outline, transcripts, tactics), cited. Where the lesson
  needs his story, rule or opinion, leave `[Matthew: your story / your rule here — <what the transcript
  hints at>]`. Never write a first-person anecdote, habit or result for him (`docs/copy-principles.md` §2).
- Offer one Pro Tip built on a real quote of his, marked as a draft.
- Address the member as "you"; no third-person narration of him outside quotes.
- Propose a concept title and say it is a proposal. Suggest cross-links the outline supports.
- One idea per lesson, short enough to finish alone in one sitting.

## Quality bar
His own statements are in the local file and the academy outline: the trainings should be the best
available and be continually updated; each module opens with the problems it solves; every training is
about why before what. The 2026-10-02 build spec adds: every claim cited or cut, simple for a first-week
member, no income or result promises. He has not said what made his rewrites good — ask before treating
this as his standard.

- When he rewrites a lesson, should its worksheet Doc be redrafted to match, relinked, or left?
- May drafts use his first-person voice for him to edit, or stay in direct address with `[Matthew: …]`?
