# Discovery Question Bank — Ecommerce (drill-down reservoir)

The drill-down layer, not the spine. Ask the 5 Questions in order first; pull from these when an answer needs depth. Don't march through all eleven as a checklist — follow the thread the owner opens. Pace applies: in Quick Pass take the must-asks and move; in Deep Dive work the dig-deepers. Technique throughout: ask, then stop talking. Push every pain past the product. Always get the time data.

## 1. Vision & OBG (comes first — the goal weighs every later answer)
Discover it, don't assume it. Stay neutral: profit, growth, scale, time-freedom, launch, or exit.
- Must: Top level, what's your vision for this brand — before I look at it? · What does winning look like in 12 months? 3 years? · If everything served one goal, what is it? · Where's the brand today vs. where you want it?
- Deeper: What would make you feel this brand "made it"? · What's already working that you want more of? · What won't you do even if it would grow the brand?

## 2. Customer / Avatar
- Must: Who's your customer (age, gender, life context)? · What's the *core* of that (tightest 10-year band)? · Describe a day in their life. · What's their pain point *outside the product you already built*? (the single most important question — keep pushing past the product until you hit a psychological/situational pain).
- Deeper: Multiple avatars? Get the split %. · Map engagement tiers — solo, small group, large event — where does the product get used? · Where do they hang out online? · What makes them loyal vs. churn?

## 3. Product Line
- Must: Walk me through every product — parents, variations, SKUs. · Price points, in-stock vs. out-of-stock right now. · What's your differentiation; why the rating you have?
- Deeper: Which 1–2 SKUs actually drive revenue? · Anything aging in storage tying up cash? · The dream products you haven't built? · Any modular/system/expansion vision that locks customers in?

## 4. Financials (the spine — route by data maturity)
Ask neutrally; thin margins or reinvestment can be a choice.
- Must: Monthly revenue now (and peak)? · CM3 in $ and %? · If not off-hand: CM1, CM2, and ad spend? · Profitability posture — comfortably profitable, breakeven, investing, or unsure?
- Deeper: Cash position, runway, debt service? · Unit economics at each price point? · Daily/monthly ad spend, and what it could be if cash allowed? · Tested pricing — what happened? · COGS and landed cost; room to lower with a different supplier?

## 5. Channel
- Must: Where do you sell — Amazon only, or multi-channel? · If multi, the split and which is growing?
- Deeper: Single-platform exposure (account health, TOS risk)? · Tried other channels — what happened? · Do you own the customer relationship anywhere, or is it all rented?

## 6. Marketing & Funnel
- Must: Walk the full funnel — impression → click → purchase → post-purchase → repeat. Where does it break? · State of listings — images, A+, video; last refreshed when? · What's PPC doing right now?
- Deeper: Post-purchase flow (insert, QR, email capture) — working or broken? · Images feature-led or benefit-led; do they convey a brand or just a product? · Do images reflect the avatar/lifestyle, or generic? · Organic vs. paid mix?

## 7. Brand & Audience
- Must: Does the brand make the customer *feel* something, or look like a generic marketplace product? · Owned audience — email, social, community — how big? · Brand story/identity, or just a name and logo?
- Deeper: Brand guidelines (logo, fonts, 3–5 core colors) applied consistently? · Done external/organic marketing — what happened? · Founder willing to be the face, or faceless/UGC? · The emotional promise — what does owning this say about the customer?

## 8. Operations & Fulfillment
- Must: How do you decide when to reorder — scheduled or reactive? · Supplier lead times and MOQs? · Fulfillment model?
- Deeper: Behind on any reorders — why? · Inventory aging / storage fees? · Order cutoff before factory closures (e.g. CNY)? · Multi-channel fulfillment complexity? · Do you know true landed cost per unit?

## 9. Team & Time (diagnostic gold)
- Must: Solo, or team/VA? · Where does your time actually go each week (export if tracked; estimates if not)? · When you focus on one area for a week, what falls apart while you're not looking?
- Deeper: Hours/week this brand actually gets from you? · Time spent that isn't moving the needle? · What would you delegate first? · Ask them to track hours per activity going forward — it's what reveals what to automate.

## 10. Tools & Systems
- Must: What's already built — spreadsheets, dashboards, automations, SOPs? · What's duplicated or held together with tape?
- Deeper: Automation maturity — manual, some tools, real systems? · Tools you pay for and underuse? · Where do you lose the most time to manual repetitive work?

## 11. Competitive Context
- Must: Top 2–3 competitors — send listings/sites. · What do category leaders do that you don't? · Where's the white space nobody does well?
- Deeper: How big are the leaders; retail presence? · Their social/community vs. yours? · Adjacent non-competing products selling to the same customer? · Any platform/tool that might subsume your differentiation (platform-risk test)?

## Closing the call
Reflect back the 3–5 quick wins you heard as hypotheses · confirm the single most urgent thing from the owner's view · agree on what data they'll send (financials, time export, competitor links) · set the time-tracking expectation · name the one thing you'll both NOT do yet, to protect focus.
